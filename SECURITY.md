# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported | End of Life |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| 1.x     | Yes       | —           |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.**

Use GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting:

https://github.com/aiascendlab/ahk20-json/security/advisories/new

Or email [lead@aiascendlab.com](mailto:lead@aiascendlab.com).

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and provide an initial
assessment within 10 business days.

## Disclosure Process

1. Report received and acknowledged
2. Fix developed privately
3. Coordinated release with advisory publication when appropriate
4. Credit given to the reporter unless anonymity is requested
